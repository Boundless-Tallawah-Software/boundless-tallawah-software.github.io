A web app is an interactive website that can help customers place orders or make requests. It can also help your business assign work to staff and track progress.

By turning repetitive steps into a clear form or guide, a web app can help make your process more consistent. Web apps can also do many of the things people expect from a smartphone app.

<{{ heading }} class="mt-8 mb-4 text-xl font-bold text-primary">Ways to use a web app
  • Let customers fill out order or request forms.
  • Guide staff through the steps needed to handle an order.
  • Assign tasks and see how work is progressing.
