A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a kind of web app that can be designed to keep working when the internet is weak or unavailable. Information already loaded on a device can remain available, and staff can continue filling out forms and saving entries.

When the connection returns, staff can review their saved entries before sending them to the business. The information is not sent until the device is back online.

<{{ heading }} class="mt-8 mb-4 text-xl font-bold text-primary">Where this can help

This can be useful for workers who take orders or collect information away from the office. Text-based tasks work well offline, while tasks involving large images or file uploads work best with a reliable internet connection.
