<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">A web app is an interactive website that can help customers place orders or make requests. It can also help your business assign work to staff and track progress.</p>

<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">By turning repetitive steps into a clear form or guide, a web app can help make your process more consistent. Web apps can also do many of the things people expect from a smartphone app.</p>

<h2>Ways to use a web app</h2>
<ul class="list-disc space-y-2 pl-6">
  <li>Let customers fill out order or request forms.</li>
  <li>Guide staff through the steps needed to handle an order.</li>
  <li>Assign tasks and see how work is progressing.</li>
</ul>
