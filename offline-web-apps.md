<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a kind of web app that can be designed to keep working when the internet is weak or unavailable. Information already loaded on a device can remain available, and staff can continue filling out forms and saving entries.</p>

<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">When the connection returns, staff can review their saved entries before sending them to the business. The information is not sent until the device is back online.</p>

<h2>Where this can help</h2>
<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">This can be useful for workers who take orders or collect information away from the office. Text-based tasks work well offline, while tasks involving large images or file uploads work best with a reliable internet connection.</p>
